Why this platform

Rhipicephalus microplus and R. decoloratus are major cattle tick pests driving acaricide resistance worldwide. The Commiphora swynnertonii benchmark screens solvent fractions (DCM / MeOH) of exudate and stem bark against larval and adult ticks, scoring mortality, phytochemical load (GC-MS), LC50/LC90 probit fits, and in-silico docking of top compounds into modelled tick acetylcholinesterase (AChE).

This site fuses those seven evidence layers with a quantum machine learning (QML) research pipeline — feature maps, quantum kernels and variational classifiers designed to complement classical analytics. The Molecule Lab lets you test any two molecules interactively; every tool is transparent about what it proves and what it does not.

How to use this workbench
  1. Molecule Lab — enter two SMILES strings, pick a tick species target, and get 2D structures, descriptors, predicted AChE affinity, a synergy index and dose guidance.
  2. Literature — inspect and externally verify each reported evidence table (docking, LC50, validation, homology-model QA).
  3. QML Methods — understand the three quantum modules, the classical baseline, and how to reproduce everything locally (branch Feat/Sheena).
  4. Ask Bot — statistical reasoning over the benchmark tables and methodology Q&A.
Molecule Lab — test a pair of molecules

RDKit (running in your browser via WebAssembly) parses your SMILES, produces 2D structures and descriptors, and a transparent ridge-QSAR surrogate estimates AChE binding affinity for two tick receptors. The synergy index blends structural complementarity (1 − Tanimoto), LogP balance, size mixing and predicted affinity gain of a hypothetical 1:1 combination (Loewe-inspired heuristic). A pre-screening estimate only — not docking, not wet-lab evidence.

Quantum Machine Learning — how it works here

QML approaches are framed to complement, not replace, classical tools. Molecular + assay descriptors are embedded into qubit amplitudes; a feature map creates a high-dimensional Hilbert-space representation and a parameterised quantum circuit (PQC) learns the target mapping. For this small, noisy benchmark the honest position is: classical baselines are the reference; quantum modules are the research frontier.

Reproduce locally

Clone git@github.com:SheenaPravin/Quantum_AgroX.git (branch Feat/Sheena), then:

pip install -r Quantum_AgroX_QML_Project/requirements.txt
cd Quantum_AgroX_QML_Project && python main.py

Quantum runs require a classical-backend simulator and are data-hungry; start with the classical baselines. QML modules ship ready but unverified against this dashboard's live surrogate.

Ask Bot — statistics & reasoning over the benchmark

A deterministic Q&A engine over the loaded tables and the project knowledge base. It answers statistical questions (best extract, LC50, affinity, synergy, dosage) and methodology questions (QML, caveats, reproduction). No free-form web retrieval — everything it says traces to this project's data.
