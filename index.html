Molecule Lab — pair test on any receptor

Enter any SMILES pair — phytochemical pesticide, herbicide, nematicide, fungicide or synthetic candidate — and name the receptor target. RDKit (WebAssembly in your browser) parses structures, computes descriptors and fingerprints, and a ridge-QSAR surrogate estimates binding affinity calibrated per target from literature anchor ligands. Draw a brand-new synthetic molecule, drop an example, or paste SMILES — the synergy index blends structural complementarity (1 − Tanimoto), LogP balance, size mixing and the predicted affinity gain of a 1:1 combination. Pre-screening hypothesis, not docking nor wet-lab proof.
